Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the tools and how your data is handled.

Are these tools really free?

Yes — all 124 tools are completely free with no sign-up, no trial and no usage limits. Use them as often as you like, on phone, tablet or computer.

Is my data private? Do you upload my files?

No. Every tool runs entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. Your images, text, passwords and numbers are processed on your own device and are never sent to or stored on a server.

How accurate is the mortgage calculator?

It uses the standard amortization formula lenders use, so the principal-and-interest figure is accurate for a fixed-rate loan. Your real payment may differ because taxes, insurance, HOA fees and lender charges vary — treat the result as a solid estimate for comparing homes and loan terms.

How much should I tip in the US and UK?

In the US, tipping is a core part of service workers' pay: 15% is the minimum for adequate service, 18–20% is standard for good service, and 22–25% rewards excellent service. In the UK, 10–12.5% is customary in restaurants, and many bills already include a service charge — check before adding more.

What GPA do I need for honors?

It varies by school, but commonly: summa cum laude ≈ 3.9+, magna cum laude ≈ 3.7+, cum laude ≈ 3.5+. A 3.0 (B average) is the minimum for many internships, scholarships and graduate programs. Always confirm your own school's thresholds.

Does the salary calculator show take-home pay?

No — it converts gross (before-tax) pay between annual and hourly figures. Take-home pay depends on federal and state taxes in the US, or income tax and National Insurance in the UK, plus pension and benefit deductions.
