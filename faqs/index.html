Quick answers about ToolNovo

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything people ask us most about ToolNovo — how the tools work, what happens to your data, and how to get help. For anything else, email support@toolnovo.net.

Using ToolNovo

Is ToolNovo really free?

Yes. Every tool on ToolNovo is completely free, with no trials, no usage limits and no hidden charges. Open a tool and use it as often as you like.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

No. There are no accounts, no registration and no email walls on ToolNovo. Every tool works the moment the page loads.

Do the tools work on my phone?

Yes. Every tool and guide is built to work on phone, tablet and computer, in any modern browser. Tools that need specific browser features — like Speech to Text — tell you clearly if your browser does not support them.

Privacy and your files

Are my files and text uploaded to a server?

No. Every ToolNovo tool runs entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. The files you open, the text you paste and the numbers you type are processed on your own device and are never sent to, or stored on, a server. Our Privacy Policy explains this in full.

What happens to a PDF or image after I use a tool?

Nothing leaves your device. Downloads are generated in your browser from your original file, and closing the page clears everything. There is no copy of your file anywhere on ToolNovo.

Does ToolNovo use cookies?

The tools themselves set no tracking cookies. A cookie-consent banner lets you accept or decline advertising cookies, and the Privacy Policy explains the Google AdSense cookies that may be used if ads are shown.

Accuracy and help

How accurate are the calculators?

Calculators use the standard published formulas for each job — the lender amortization formula for mortgages, Mifflin–St Jeor for calories, the standard 4.0 scale for GPA, and so on. Results are estimates for general information; our Disclaimer explains the limits, and important decisions should always be confirmed with a professional.

A tool gave a result I think is wrong. What should I do?

Tell us via the Contact page — include the tool name and the exact numbers or file you used, so we can reproduce the problem and fix it. Reports of wrong results are our top priority.

Can I suggest a new tool?

Yes — email support@toolnovo.net or use the Contact page and choose ‘Suggest a new tool’. Tell us the problem the tool would solve; the most-requested tools get built first.

Can I use ToolNovo results in my own work?

Yes. Calculations, conversions and files you produce with the tools are yours to use, including in commercial work. The site’s written guides are copyrighted — please link to them rather than copying them wholesale, as set out in our Terms of Service.
