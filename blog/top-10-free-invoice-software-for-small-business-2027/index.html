Top 10 Free Invoice Software for Small Business in 2027

⚠ Please note

General information only — not financial advice. Figures in this article are examples to explain how the maths works. Your own rates, fees and terms will differ, so confirm everything with your lender, insurer or a licensed adviser before you decide.

Getting paid on time is the difference between a business that grows and one that survives. A late invoice is not a small admin slip — it is cash that should already be in your bank account. Yet many small businesses still invoice from a word processor document and only notice a missing payment weeks later.

The fix costs nothing. Genuinely free invoice software now covers everything a small business needs: professional templates, automatic reminders, online payment links and a record of who has paid and who has not. This guide compares ten free invoice tools for small businesses in 2027, each with a real free plan, not just a trial.

1. Wave

Wave is the most generous free invoicing tool here: unlimited invoices, clients and estimates, with no monthly fee. Brand invoices with your logo, set up recurring invoices for regular clients, and send reminders when a due date passes.

Wave also includes free accounting and receipt scanning, so invoices and income stay in one place. You only pay a processing fee when a client pays by card or bank transfer.

2. Zoho Invoice

Zoho Invoice is completely free for small businesses, with no cap on invoices or clients. It covers the full cycle: estimates, invoices, payment links, credit notes, expenses and client statements, in an interface you learn in minutes.

Its best feature is automation: reminders on your schedule, recurring invoices that send themselves, and a client portal for online payment. For a business billing the same clients monthly, that alone is worth switching.

3. Invoice Ninja

Invoice Ninja is the strongest free choice for control over how your invoices look and work. The free plan covers up to 20 clients and includes templates, quotes, expenses, time tracking and payment links.

It is also the most flexible option: self-host it to keep your data in-house, or use the hosted version and send your first invoice within the hour. Every invoice shows when it was sent, viewed and paid.

4. Square Invoices

Square Invoices is the natural pick if you already take card payments in person. The free plan lets you create and send unlimited invoices, and you only pay a processing fee when a client pays by card — no monthly charge.

Its standout feature is speed: clients pay from a link in the invoice, and the money lands on the usual schedule. Automatic reminders and scheduled invoices suit service businesses that invoice on the job.

5. PayPal Invoicing

PayPal Invoicing is the easiest tool to start with, because most businesses already have a PayPal account. There is no monthly fee or setup: create an invoice in minutes, send it by email, and your client pays with PayPal, a card or their bank balance.

It suits businesses with clients across countries: they pay in their own currency, and you can invoice in over 25 currencies. You only pay a fee when you receive a payment, so an unpaid invoice costs you nothing.

6. Invoicely

Invoicely is a simple, fast tool that does the basics very well. The free plan covers up to three clients and includes unlimited invoices, estimates, expenses and payment links, with a dashboard showing what is due and overdue.

It is the best pick for the smallest businesses — a side business or freelancer with a handful of regulars — because there is nothing to configure or learn. Paid plans only matter once you grow past those first few clients.

7. Hiveage

Hiveage is built for small teams that send invoices regularly. Its free plan includes unlimited invoices and estimates, recurring billing, online payments and a client portal, plus file attachments so the work and the bill travel together.

What sets it apart is how it handles teams: members can be assigned to different clients, and the dashboard shows who owes what. For a two- or three-person business, that shared view replaces the spreadsheet everyone keeps separately.

8. Billdu

Billdu keeps invoicing simple and mobile-first. The free plan covers a small number of clients and includes invoice creation, estimates, expenses and a mobile app that lets you send an invoice from a job site before you leave it.

Its most useful feature is the payment reminder: Billdu chases overdue invoices automatically, with polite reminders on your schedule. If you do the work and the admin, that is one chore you can stop thinking about.

9. Akaunting

Akaunting is free, open-source invoicing and accounting software — the only tool here you can run entirely on your own server, with no vendor in the middle. It covers invoices, bills, expenses, deposits and bank accounts, with no cap on clients or invoices.

It is the best choice for a business that wants full ownership of its data. The trade-off is setup — you need someone comfortable installing software — but once it runs, there is no subscription at all.

10. Zervant

Zervant is designed for small businesses and freelancers who want professional invoices without any learning curve. The free plan covers a small number of customers and includes invoices, quotes, expense tracking and a client list, in an interface that feels more like an email app than accounting software.

Its standout feature is the payment reminder system: overdue invoices are chased automatically, and you can see which clients pay on time and which need a nudge. For an owner who would rather do the work than chase money, that is exactly the right help.

How to Choose the Right One

Start from how you actually bill. If you invoice the same clients every month, pick a tool with recurring invoices and automatic reminders, like Zoho Invoice or Wave. If clients are in different countries, PayPal Invoicing removes the currency friction. If you take card payments in person, Square Invoices keeps everything in one system.

Before you commit, send two or three real invoices through your shortlisted tool. Check the invoice looks professional on a phone and the payment link works. For a quick practice run, ToolNovo's free invoice generator creates a clean invoice in minutes with no sign-up.

Common Mistakes Small Businesses Make With Invoicing

Sending invoices late. The longer you wait to invoice, the longer you wait to be paid. Send it the same day the work is done, while the value is fresh in the client's mind.

Leaving out payment terms. Every invoice should state when payment is due, how to pay, and what happens if it is late. A due date means nothing if it is not written on the invoice.

Not chasing overdue invoices. Most late payments are forgotten invoices, not refusals. A polite reminder after the due date recovers more money than any new sale, so switch reminders on and let the tool chase.

Changing invoice number formats. Sequential numbers keep records clean and make a missing payment easy to spot. Pick one format and let the software number invoices for you.

Keeping invoices in a word processor. A document cannot tell you who has paid, who is overdue, or how much you are owed. Even the simplest invoicing tool turns that guessing into a dashboard you can read in ten seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is free invoice software really free?

Yes — every tool here offers a free plan with no card required, not just a trial. Most free plans limit clients or advanced features, and most charge a processing fee only when a client pays by card. Sending invoices is free.

Do I need invoice software if I only send a few invoices a month?

Even a few invoices a month benefit: professional templates, automatic numbering, payment reminders and a record of who has paid. It takes the same effort as sending a document, and it removes the two most common problems — forgotten invoices and forgotten follow-ups.

Can I switch invoice tools later without losing my records?

Almost always. Every tool here exports invoices and client lists to a spreadsheet, so you can move your history to another tool. Pick the tool that fits today and switch when you outgrow it — your records come with you.

Key Takeaway

The best free invoice software gets invoices sent the same day and chases the late ones without you thinking about it — and for most small businesses that means Wave for unlimited free invoicing, Zoho Invoice for automation, Square Invoices for card payments, and PayPal Invoicing for international clients. Pick one, send your next invoice through it, and switch the reminders on: getting paid on time is a system, not a hope.
