About the Favicon Generator

The favicon generator creates the small icon that appears in browser tabs, bookmarks and phone home screens. Build one from a letter or emoji with your brand colour, or turn your own logo image into a proper icon set: PNG files at 16, 32, 48 and 180 pixels, plus a classic favicon.ico containing the standard sizes.

How to use the Favicon Generator — step by step

  1. Choose a mode: Text / Emoji or Upload Image.
  2. For text mode, type one or two characters or an emoji and pick a background colour.
  3. For image mode, choose a square logo image for the cleanest result.
  4. Preview the icon, then download the sizes you need — including the ICO file for your site root.

Worked example

Type the letter 'T' on an emerald background and download the set: a 16×16 for browser tabs, 32×32 for retina tabs, 180×180 for Apple touch icons and a favicon.ico bundling the classic sizes — a complete favicon kit from one letter.

When to use it

Use it when launching a website or blog, refreshing a brand, or replacing a default globe icon so your tab stands out among twenty open tabs.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using a detailed photo — at 16 pixels only a bold letter, simple shape or strong emoji survives.
  • Forgetting the apple-touch-icon size (180×180) if you want a nice home-screen icon on iPhones.
  • Uploading a wide rectangular logo: crop it square first or it will be squeezed.

Favicon Generator — frequently asked questions

Which files does my website actually need?

At minimum favicon.ico in the site root. Modern sites add a 32×32 PNG and a 180×180 Apple touch icon — this generator makes all of them.

Is the ICO a real ICO file?

Yes. It is a genuine ICO container bundling PNG images at 16, 32 and 48 pixels, the format browsers have used for decades.
