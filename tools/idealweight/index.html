About the Ideal Weight Calculator

The ideal weight calculator shows the healthy weight range for your height in two ways: the BMI 18.5–24.9 band used by health services, and the classic Devine formula used in medicine. It gives a range, not a single magic number, because healthy bodies vary.

How to use the Ideal Weight Calculator — step by step

  1. Select male or female (used by the Devine formula).
  2. Enter your height in feet and inches.
  3. Read your healthy range in pounds and kilograms, plus the Devine estimate.

Worked example

A man who is 5 ft 10 in has a healthy BMI range of about 129–174 lb (58.5–79 kg). The Devine formula puts his 'ideal' at about 166 lb (75.3 kg) — near the top of the range, which is normal for that older formula.

When to use it

Use it to set a realistic target range for weight loss or gain, or as a reference alongside BMI, waist measurement and your clinician's advice.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Aiming for the very bottom of the range — the whole band is healthy, and the middle suits most builds.
  • Applying adult ranges to teenagers, whose healthy weight depends on growth and age.
  • Ignoring build and muscle: broad or athletic frames can be healthy above the range.

Ideal Weight Calculator — frequently asked questions

Why do the two methods differ?

The BMI band is a wide statistical range for populations. The Devine formula gives one point estimate from 1974 medical practice. The range is the more useful guide.

Should my ideal weight change with age?

The ranges stay the same, but body composition changes with age. Many clinicians focus more on waist size, strength and metabolic health in older adults.
