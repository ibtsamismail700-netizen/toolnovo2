About the Tip Calculator & Bill Splitter

This tip calculator works out a fair tip and splits the total evenly, so nobody at the table has to do mental maths. Tipping customs vary by place: in the United States and Canada tipping is a core part of service pay, while in the UK it is smaller and a service charge is often already included.

How to use the Tip Calculator & Bill Splitter — step by step

  1. Choose USD, GBP or CAD.
  2. Enter the bill amount before tip.
  3. Tap a tip percentage (10–25%) or type a custom percentage.
  4. Drag the slider to set how many people are splitting the bill, then read the per-person amount.

Worked example

An $86.40 dinner bill with an 18% tip gives a $15.55 tip and a $101.95 total. Split between 2 people, each person pays $50.98 — $43.20 for food and $7.78 of tip each.

When to use it

Use it at restaurants, bars, cafes, salons and for delivery drivers — anywhere a group bill needs splitting fairly, or when you travel and are unsure of local tipping norms.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Tipping on the after-tax total when you meant the pre-tax amount — the difference is small but adds up.
  • Double-tipping in the UK when a 12.5% service charge is already on the bill — check first.
  • Splitting only the food and forgetting the tip, drinks or shared starters.

Tip Calculator & Bill Splitter — frequently asked questions

How much should I tip in the US and Canada?

15% is the minimum for adequate service, 18–20% is standard for good service, and 22–25% rewards excellent service. For delivery, $3–$5 or 10–15% is common.

Do I tip in the UK?

10–12.5% in restaurants is customary, but many bills already include a service charge. Tipping in pubs is optional.
