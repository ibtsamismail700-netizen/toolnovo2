About the Macro Calculator

The macro calculator splits your daily calorie target into protein, carbohydrate and fat grams for your chosen eating style — balanced, high-protein, low-carb or keto. It is the natural second step after the Calorie Calculator: calories set the total, macros decide what the total is made of.

How to use the Macro Calculator — step by step

  1. Enter your daily calorie target.
  2. Choose a diet style — the percentages update the split.
  3. Enter your body weight to check protein per pound.
  4. Build meals to the gram targets, prioritising the protein number first.

Worked example

At 2,200 kcal on the balanced split (30/40/30), targets are 165 g protein, 220 g carbs and 73 g fat. For a 180 lb person that protein figure is 0.92 g per lb — right in the muscle-building range.

When to use it

Use it when starting a diet, planning meal prep, or troubleshooting a plateau where calories are right but protein is low.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Hitting calories but missing protein badly — protein protects muscle in a deficit and controls hunger.
  • Treating keto percentages as a starting point for beginners — most people do better on balanced or high-protein first.
  • Tracking perfectly for three days then not at all — a rough average over a week beats perfection and collapse.

Macro Calculator — frequently asked questions

How much protein do I really need?

About 0.7–1 g per lb of body weight for active people and muscle goals; around 0.36 g per lb is the minimum for sedentary adults.

Do I need to hit all three numbers exactly?

Protein matters most, calories second. Carbs and fat can flex against each other within your calorie total.
