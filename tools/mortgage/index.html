About the Mortgage Calculator

A mortgage calculator estimates your monthly home-loan payment before you talk to a lender. It splits the payment into principal and interest, and can add property tax (US), Council Tax (UK), home insurance and HOA or condo fees so you see the true monthly cost of a home.

How to use the Mortgage Calculator — step by step

  1. Choose your currency: USD ($), GBP (£) or CAD ($).
  2. Enter the home price and your down payment — the loan amount is the difference.
  3. Enter the interest rate and loan term (30, 25, 20 or 15 years).
  4. Add yearly tax and insurance if you want the full monthly cost, then press Calculate payment.

Worked example

A $450,000 home with a $90,000 down payment leaves a $360,000 loan. At 6.5% over 30 years, principal and interest is about $2,275 per month. Add $5,400/year property tax ($450/month) and $1,800/year insurance ($150/month) and the full payment is about $2,875 per month, with roughly $459,000 of interest paid over the full term.

When to use it

Use it when comparing homes, deciding how much down payment to save, or checking whether a shorter term is affordable. A 15-year term costs more each month but usually saves a very large amount of interest.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Forgetting taxes, insurance and HOA fees — the real payment can be hundreds more per month.
  • Comparing homes on price alone instead of on total monthly cost.
  • Assuming the rate you see advertised is the rate you will qualify for — your credit score and deposit size matter.

Mortgage Calculator — frequently asked questions

How much down payment do I need?

In the US many buyers put down 3–20%, in the UK typically 5–25%, and in Canada at least 5% for homes under $500,000. Below 20% in the US or Canada usually means extra mortgage insurance costs.

Why is so much of my early payment interest?

Interest is charged on the full balance, which is highest at the start. As the balance falls, more of each payment goes to principal.
