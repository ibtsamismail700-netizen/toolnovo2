About the Unit Converter

The unit converter translates between imperial and metric measurements — the everyday conversions needed between the United States (imperial), the United Kingdom (a mix) and Canada (officially metric). Length, weight, volume, area, speed and temperature are all covered, including UK versus US gallons, which are genuinely different.

How to use the Unit Converter — step by step

  1. Choose a category: length, weight, volume, area or speed.
  2. Enter your value and pick the 'from' and 'to' units.
  3. Read the converted result instantly.
  4. For temperature, use the dedicated °F to °C (or reverse) converter at the bottom of the panel.

Worked example

10 feet is 3.05 metres. 170 pounds is 77.1 kg, or 12 stone 2 lb in UK terms. A US gallon is 3.785 litres but a UK gallon is 4.546 litres — a 20% difference. And 72°F is 22.2°C, a pleasant room temperature.

When to use it

Use it for recipes, DIY and construction, travel, shipping weights, fitness, schoolwork and any time a product spec arrives in the 'wrong' units.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Confusing US and UK (imperial) gallons and pints — they differ by about 20%.
  • Confusing a US ton (2,000 lb) with a metric tonne (1,000 kg) or a UK long ton.
  • Converting temperature with a ratio — °F to °C needs the subtract-32 step, which the temperature box handles.

Unit Converter — frequently asked questions

How many centimetres are in an inch?

Exactly 2.54 cm. A foot is 30.48 cm and a mile is 1.609 kilometres.

Why does the UK use both systems?

The UK is officially metric but still uses miles on roads, pints for beer and milk, and stones for body weight — so UK users convert in both directions daily.
