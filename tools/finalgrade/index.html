About the Final Grade Calculator

The final grade calculator answers the question every student asks in exam season: what do I need on the final to get the grade I want? Enter your current grade, your target, and how much the final exam counts — it also shows the score needed for every grade band and what a specific final score would give you.

How to use the Final Grade Calculator — step by step

  1. Enter your current course percentage.
  2. Enter the overall grade you want.
  3. Enter the final exam's weight as a percentage of the course.
  4. Optionally enter a predicted or actual final score to see your resulting grade.

Worked example

You have 88% going into a final worth 25%, and you want 90% overall. You need (90 − 88 × 0.75) ÷ 0.25 = 96% on the final. Wanting 85% overall instead needs only 76% — a far calmer target.

When to use it

Use it to set a realistic revision target, decide where to focus effort across courses, and check whether a target is still mathematically possible before you burn out chasing it.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using your current grade without checking it is weighted the same way as the syllabus says.
  • Forgetting other remaining work — projects and homework still to come also move the final grade.
  • Assuming you need 100%: often the maths shows your target is already secured or needs a surprisingly modest score.

Final Grade Calculator — frequently asked questions

What if the calculator says 'not possible'?

It means even 100% on the final cannot reach that target — it also shows the maximum overall grade still available, so you can aim at the best realistic outcome.

How is the needed score calculated?

Needed = (Target − Current × (1 − Weight)) ÷ Weight, where Weight is the final's share of the course.
