About the QR Code Generator

The QR code generator turns any link or text into a scannable QR code you can download as a PNG. Restaurants, shops, event organisers and creators use QR codes on menus, flyers, packaging and business cards to take people from print to a web page in one scan.

How to use the QR Code Generator — step by step

  1. Type or paste the link or text the code should open.
  2. Choose a size — 300 px for screens, 500–800 px for print.
  3. Pick an error-correction level (High for print that may get scuffed).
  4. Generate, test-scan it with your phone, then download the PNG.

Worked example

Entering https://example.com/menu at 500 × 500 with High error correction produces a crisp code for a table menu that still scans even if part of the print is covered by a sticker or worn — up to about 30% damage is recoverable at that level.

When to use it

Use it for menus, Wi-Fi sharing, event posters, product packaging, business cards, payment links and connecting print marketing to your website.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Printing too small — keep printed codes at least 2 cm (about 0.8 in) wide and test at arm's length.
  • Low contrast: dark code on a light background scans best. Avoid reversed or colourful codes without testing.
  • Linking to a page that is not mobile-friendly — nearly every scan happens on a phone.

QR Code Generator — frequently asked questions

Do QR codes expire?

No. A code generated here simply contains your text or link, so it works forever — as long as the destination link stays live.

What size should I print?

At least 2 × 2 cm for close-up scanning, and bigger the further away the scanner will be. A poster code should be 10 cm or more.
