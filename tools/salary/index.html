About the Salary to Hourly Calculator

The salary to hourly calculator converts pay between a yearly salary and an hourly wage. It is essential when comparing a salaried job offer with an hourly or contract role, or when working out what your time is really worth as a freelancer.

How to use the Salary to Hourly Calculator — step by step

  1. Pick the direction: Salary to Hourly, or Hourly to Salary.
  2. Choose USD, GBP or CAD.
  3. Enter the salary or hourly rate.
  4. Set your hours per week and weeks worked per year — the breakdown updates instantly.

Worked example

A $65,000 salary at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks equals $31.25 per hour, $250 per day, $1,250 per week and about $5,417 per month. In the UK, a £38,000 salary on a 37.5-hour contract is about £19.49 per hour.

When to use it

Use it to compare job offers fairly, price freelance work, check overtime value, or see what a part-time schedule would actually pay.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using 52 weeks when you take unpaid leave — use 48–50 weeks for an honest rate.
  • Forgetting that UK full-time is often 37.5 hours, not 40 — the hourly rate changes a lot.
  • Comparing gross salary with take-home pay — this tool converts gross (before tax) figures only.

Salary to Hourly Calculator — frequently asked questions

Does this show take-home pay?

No. It converts gross pay before income tax, US Social Security, UK National Insurance, Canadian CPP/EI and pension deductions.

What is a good hourly rate for a $50,000 salary?

At 40 hours a week, $50,000 a year is $24.04 per hour.
