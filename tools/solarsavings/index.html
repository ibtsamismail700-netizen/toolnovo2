About the Solar Panel Savings Estimator

The solar panel savings estimator projects how much electricity a rooftop system will generate in your climate, what that power is worth at your electricity price, and how many years until the system pays for itself. It is a screening estimate, clearly labelled as such — real quotes depend on your roof.

How to use the Solar Panel Savings Estimator — step by step

  1. Enter the system size in kW (a typical home system is 4–8 kW).
  2. Enter peak sun hours for your area using the guide in the tool.
  3. Enter your electricity price and the installed cost after incentives.
  4. Read yearly generation, yearly saving and payback years.

Worked example

A 6 kW system in a 4.5-sun-hour region generates about 8,350 kWh a year after losses. At $0.16/kWh that saves roughly $1,336 a year, paying back a $15,000 installed cost in about 11 years — faster where prices or sunshine are higher.

When to use it

Use it to screen whether solar deserves real quotes, compare quote sizes, and understand installer payback claims before signing anything.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Believing any payback figure without the assumptions: sun hours, export rates and price rises change everything.
  • Assuming every generated kWh saves the full retail price — power exported while you are out often earns far less.
  • Ignoring roof direction, angle and shading, which installers assess and this estimate cannot.

Solar Panel Savings Estimator — frequently asked questions

What incentives exist?

The US has offered a federal residential clean-energy credit, the UK pays for exports via the Smart Export Guarantee, and Canada has run grant and loan programs — all change over time, so verify current rules before buying.

Do panels work in the UK and Canada?

Yes — modern panels work on daylight, not heat. Generation is lower in winter and at northern latitudes, which the sun-hours input accounts for.
