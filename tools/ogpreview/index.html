About the Social Share Preview Tool

The social share preview shows how a link will actually look when shared on Facebook, X and LinkedIn — image, title, description and domain — using the Open Graph values you enter. Shares are earned in the feed, and a missing image or a truncated title silently halves clicks.

How to use the Social Share Preview Tool — step by step

  1. Enter the page title, description and URL as your Open Graph tags will serve them.
  2. Paste the social image URL — 1200 × 630 px is the standard.
  3. Compare the three platform cards and fix anything cut off, blurry or vague.
  4. Set the matching tags with the Meta Tag Generator, then re-share to refresh platform caches.

Worked example

A guide shared with a clear 1200 × 630 image and the title 'Best Coffee Shops in New York' shows as a large, tappable card on all three platforms. The same link without an image collapses to a small grey thumbnail that most people scroll past.

When to use it

Use it before launching content you intend to promote, after fixing wrong share images, and when auditing evergreen posts.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Judging by the image alone — on X and LinkedIn the title does most of the work and truncates fast.
  • Linking an image that is huge (slow) or tiny (blurry); keep it under about 1 MB at 1200 × 630.
  • Forgetting that platforms cache: after fixing tags, use each platform's debugger or a fresh share to pull the new version.

Social Share Preview Tool — frequently asked questions

Why does my share show old information?

Platforms cache Open Graph data. Facebook and LinkedIn offer sharing debuggers to force a refresh; X usually updates within days.

What if I have no image?

Create a simple branded 1200 × 630 graphic with the title text — it outperforms a generic stock photo.
