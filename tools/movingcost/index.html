About the Moving Cost Estimator

The moving cost estimator gives a realistic price range for a house move based on home size, distance and service level — from a DIY truck to a full packing service. Moving is one of life's most under-budgeted events; this replaces a guess with a range.

How to use the Moving Cost Estimator — step by step

  1. Choose your home size and enter the distance in miles.
  2. Pick the service level: DIY truck, professional movers, or full service.
  3. Read the mid estimate and the low–high range.
  4. Use the range to compare at least three written quotes.

Worked example

A 1-bedroom move of 450 miles with professional movers estimates around $1,600, within a range of roughly $1,275–$2,000. The same move with a DIY truck drops to around $600–700 plus your fuel, time and lifting.

When to use it

Use it when budgeting a relocation, deciding between DIY and movers, or negotiating a job relocation package.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Booking the cheapest quote without checking insurance, reviews and what is excluded.
  • Forgetting supplies, tips, cleaning, storage gaps and overlap rent or mortgage days.
  • Moving on a summer weekend or month-end — the most expensive slots in all three countries.

Moving Cost Estimator — frequently asked questions

How far ahead should I book?

4–8 weeks for movers in peak season; DIY trucks 2–4 weeks.

Are estimates binding?

Only a written, in-home or video-survey quote is close to binding. Phone estimates based on your description often rise on the day.
